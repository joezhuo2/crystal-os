Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course students should be able to:
Demonstrate skill in manipulating functions and performing calculations such as partial fraction decompositions and expressing piecewise defined functions in terms of Heaviside functions
Read and use mathematical definitions
Demonstrate skill in evaluation of limits, derivatives and integrals
Use integration to calculate areas between and lengths of curves, find average values of functions, and calculating volumes of solids of revolution
Use limits and derivatives to sketch graphs of functions or solve optimization problems
Use integration to calculate areas enclosed by segments of curves in polar coordinates, and lengths of such curve segments
Sketch curves described in polar coordinates

Tutorial Assignments - Distributed, submitted, and returned during weekly tutorials.

Each tutorial will feature a graded problem set designed to get you thinking at a deeper level about the material. You may also see some new content that augments what was discussed in class. It is recommended that you prepare for the tutorials in advance by reviewing your notes from the previous week of class and attempting the practice problems posted on LEARN and in the textbook. 

You may solve the problems in groups, or individually if you prefer. If working in a group, each group member must write and submit their own solutions in their own words. Exchanging ideas is encouraged, but the final submission must be your own work. You are permitted to use your personal notes, any resources on the MATH 117 LEARN page, and a scientific (non-graphing) calculator. No other resources are permitted. The Tutorial Assignments must be handed in by the end of the tutorial; no extension on submission is allowed. We will take the best 7 out of 9 assignments when computing your final grade. If a student misses a tutorial (due to illness, etc.), then that missed assignment will be counted as one of the dropped assignments. Students who miss more than 2 assignments will have the weight(s) of those assignments transferred to the final exam. It is the responsibility of the student to inform their instructor if they missed more than 2 assignments to ensure that the assignment weight(s) will be transferred to the final exam.

Notes on Midterm and Final Exam:

  • There will be no make-up midterm. Students who miss the midterm and provide proper documentation will have the weight of the midterm transferred to the final exam. Otherwise a grade of zero will be awarded to those who miss the midterm.

  • Absence from the final exam may result in a grade of INC or DNW at the discretion of the instructor.
